Visual tutorial: mockup (I-34)

This is a proposal to look at, not a decision. On the start screen a How it works button opens an overlay with eight short steps. Each step has a picture of the real trainer screen with the part it talks about highlighted, and two to four lines of text in the words of CLASS_USE.md. Back, Next and Skip work in the frame below. The animations stop when the phone asks for reduced motion, and every picture still makes sense without them.
