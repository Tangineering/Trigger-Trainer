# Using the Trainer in Class

## Before class
- Scan-to-open QR code for the site URL on a slide or handout.
- Check volume on a demo phone: hold **Test sound** on the start screen (also in Settings). iPhones: Silent mode off.
- Have students hold **Test sound** and turn their volume down before starting; the alarms are loud.
- Pick starting thresholds in Settings on your demo phone and tell students the same numbers if they need tuning.
- For a quick looser or stricter class setting, use Settings → Movement alarm → **Overall sensitivity**: Low, Default and High set the rotation, linear and max-drift sliders to fixed values (Low 32.5 °/s, 1.95 m/s², 6.5°; Default 25 °/s, 1.5 m/s², 5°; High 17.5 °/s, 1.05 m/s², 3.5°). You can fine-tune any of the three sliders afterwards; the drop-down then shows **Custom**.
- A phone keeps only the settings that were changed on it; every other setting follows the current version of the page.
  **Restore defaults** (Settings) removes all changes on that phone.

## Student setup (about 1 minute)
1. Open the link, tap **Start**. iPhone: tap **Allow** for motion access.
2. Turn the phone sideways. Left-handed: Settings → Shooting hand → Left.
3. Hold the phone so the screen faces your trigger-finger side, muzzle toward the end marked MUZZLE.
4. **Point the muzzle downrange and tap Set downrange** (under the muzzle). Until then the 180
   check is off. Tap it again to reset; if the page goes to the background, set it again.
5. Lay the index finger flat on the dashed strip. It turns green and plays the OK tone.

## Drills
- **Ready and move:** finger on the frame, move between "targets" around the room. No alarm should sound.
- **Finger off = freeze:** lift the finger to the trigger only when the phone is still. Any movement → alarm.
  Slow movement counts too: once the muzzle has drifted more than 5° by default (Settings, "Max muzzle drift off the
  frame") from where it pointed when the finger left the frame, the alarm sounds, also on the trigger and
  during a press. Twisting the phone about the barrel axis does not count. Finger back on the frame clears it.
- **Press, don't slap:** slow rearward press; watch the blue trace and the shot dot. Green ring = clean.
- **Reset and follow-up:** after a shot, let the trigger forward until it re-arms, press again.
- **Back to the frame:** after every string, finger flat on the frame → OK tone before moving.
- **One finger at a time:** only the finger that touched first counts; a second finger on the frame or the trigger is
  ignored until it is lifted, so the same finger must leave the frame to reach the trigger.
- **Finger on the trigger is not a rest:** resting the fingertip on the trigger and moving alarms, and so does
  moving during a press. One short jerk during the press is graded, not alarmed; anything longer, or
  several wobbles adding up to more than about a third of a second (300 ms by default), alarms. A jerk
  that moves the muzzle more than the drift limit (5° by default) alarms as well.
- **180:** turn and move freely with the finger on the frame. Past 90° from downrange a lower "wail" alarm
  sounds even with the finger on the frame. "Muzzle N° from downrange" under the muzzle shows how close they are.

## Timed drills (Bill Drill, Low Ready, Box Drill, Transitions)
The **Drill** button on the start screen (above **Start**) opens the drill picker. **Freestyle** is the trainer
described above, with no timer. Pick a drill, check the diagram, tap **Choose**; the phone remembers the choice.
All Freestyle rules stay on during a drill: movement alarm, drift limit and 180 rule, except the Transitions window
(D-111: from the first shot to the tenth, movement with the finger on the trigger does not alarm).
- **Bill Drill:** tap **Start**, finger flat on the frame. After a random 1–4 s the start beep sounds (a high
  shot-timer beep; it only plays with the finger on the frame). Fire six shots as fast as the phone stays still,
  with a full trigger reset each time, then put the finger back on the frame: the completion tone plays and the
  results open.
- **Low Ready** (five reps): competition-style low ready — the muzzle stays pointed downrange and the gun is
  lowered, so there is no muzzle-angle check and **Set downrange** is not needed (the 180 rule still works if it is
  set). Tap **Start**, finger flat on the frame. After a random 2–5 s the start beep sounds: raise and fire two, then
  put the finger back on the frame. Keep it there: 750 ms later three short beeps mean "back to low ready" (lifting
  the finger before then restarts the wait; with only one shot fired there are no beeps, fire again). 2–5 s after
  the three beeps the next rep's start beep sounds. After rep 5 the completion tone plays instead and the results
  open: for each rep the reaction (beep to first shot), the split (first to second shot) and the grades, then the
  total (first beep to the end), the average and best reaction over the reps with no alarm, early shots, "With
  alarm" (an alarm during a rep, between reps or still sounding at a beep marks the run and that rep; its reaction
  is left out of the average), and the best average reaction of the session among runs with no alarm. **Again**
  starts a new five-rep run; **Change drill** goes back to the start screen.
- **Box Drill** (five positions, student-paced movement): mark four spots on the floor, for example the corners of a
  2–3 m square, numbered 1 to 4 going round. Every position faces downrange: the student walks sideways, forwards or
  backwards, never turns the muzzle off the range, and keeps the finger flat on the frame while walking (touching the
  trigger while walking alarms). Tap **Start** at spot 1, finger on the frame. After a random 1–4 s the start beep
  sounds: fire two or more, then put the finger back on the frame. Three short beeps (just after the OK tone) mean
  "move to the next spot". The phone cannot see the walk: the cue comes when the finger is back on the frame after two
  or more shots, so with only one shot there are no beeps, fire again. Spots 2, 3, 4, then back at spot 1: after two or
  more shots there and the finger on the frame the completion tone plays and the results open: for each position the
  time (from the beep or the previous cue to the first shot there: the move plus the settle), the split(s) and
  grades, then the total (beep to the last shot), early shots, "With alarm", and the best total of the session among
  runs with no alarm. **Again** starts a new run; **Change drill** goes back to the start screen.
- **Transitions** (ten shots, two at each target): set up two or three targets in view, for example left, middle and
  right (left–middle–right–middle–left is a good order; the phone does not know where the targets are, so any order
  works). Start from low ready as in Low Ready: no muzzle-angle check, **Set downrange** not needed. Tap **Start**,
  finger flat on the frame. After a random 2–5 s the start beep sounds: raise and fire two at the first target, then
  swing to the next target **with the finger on the trigger** and fire two there, and so on: five pairs, ten shots.
  This is the only drill that allows movement with the finger on the trigger, and only from the first shot to the
  tenth. Before the first shot (raising from low ready) and after the tenth the normal rules apply: the finger on the
  trigger while moving alarms. Lifting the finger off the trigger mid-string (not onto the frame) is off the frame
  again: moving then alarms as usual. The 180 rule still applies throughout when downrange is set. After the tenth
  shot put the finger back on the frame: the completion tone plays and the results open: first shot (beep to first
  shot), the five pair splits (first to second shot of each pair), the four transitions (last shot of a pair to the
  first shot of the next), the average transition, the total (beep to the tenth shot), the grade of each shot (each
  graded from where the muzzle pointed when that press started, so the swing is not counted), early shots, "With
  alarm", and the best total of the session among runs with no alarm. **Again** starts a new run; **Change drill**
  goes back to the start screen.
- **Bill Drill results:** first shot (beep to first shot), the five splits, total (beep to sixth shot), the grade of each
  shot, early shots (fired before the beep; not timed), "With alarm" if an alarm went off during the run or was
  still sounding at the beep (shots fired during an alarm show as "alarm", not graded), and the best total of the session among runs with no alarm.
  **Again** starts a new run with a new random delay; **Change drill** goes back to the start screen.
- Opening Settings, the summary, switching away from the page or holding the phone in portrait cancels the run
  (no tone); the line under the status says so, and the next run starts once the finger is back on the frame.
- Results last for this session (**New session** or **Reset stats** clears them); nothing is saved.

## Reading the target
- Dot in the green ring: clean press.
- Amber: the muzzle moved. Red: jerked. The label gives the direction (e.g. "low-left").
- Only valid once the axis check has been done on that phone (Settings → Aim trace axis check).

## End of the session
- **⚙︎ → Finish** shows the session summary: time, shots, shots fired during an alarm, how many were clean /
  moved / jerked, average and steadiest movement during the press, most common miss direction, movement alarms
  and 180 violations. Shots fired during an alarm count in the total and on the plot, but the clean / moved /
  jerked figures and the movement figures cover only the shots fired with no alarm. Students can show it to you
  or screenshot it; nothing is saved.
- **Keep training** goes back with the stats kept. **New session** clears them and returns to the start screen.

## Checking the shot vibration (Android, WQ-023)
Turn on Settings → "Show sensor readout on the trainer screen". The readout has a line
`vib: <result> (pattern <ms>)` for the last time the page asked the phone to vibrate (a shot asks for
pattern `45`; alarms and trigger reset ask for longer ones, so read it straight after a shot):
- `vib: true` — Chrome accepted the request. If nothing was felt, the phone's own settings muted it
  (touch/haptic vibration off, Do not disturb, battery saver).
- `vib: false` — Chrome refused it (typically no user activation yet, fullscreen, or page not focused).
- `vib: n/a` — the browser has no vibration (all iPhones; expected).
- `vib: threw` — the call raised an error. `vib: -` — the page has not asked to vibrate yet.
