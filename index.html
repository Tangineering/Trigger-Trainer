Trigger Discipline Trainer

⚠ Loud sounds. This trainer plays loud alarm tones. Check your volume before you start: hold Test sound and set the volume here and on the phone. Keep the phone away from your ears.
  1. Turn the phone sideways. The screen is the side of the pistol; the muzzle points toward the arrow.
  2. Lay your index finger flat along the frame (the dashed strip). With it there you can move freely.
  3. For the 180 check: point the muzzle downrange and tap Set downrange (bottom right, next to the target). Until then the 180 check is off; everything else works.
  4. Only when the "sights are on target": move your fingertip to the trigger and pull it back to fire. The phone must stay still.
  5. Moving the phone while your finger is off the frame sounds an alarm until your finger is back on the frame.
  6. Returning your finger to the frame plays the OK tone.

180 rule
